Campus Parking Map

Introducing our new UC Merced Interactive Campus Map! Navigating your way around parking lots on campus can be frustrating and time-consuming, but with our new map, it's easier to locate parking lots. The map is designed to be user-friendly and interactive, allowing you to view the parking lots and where they are located. You are able to view which lots and names are available along with information about the lots.
